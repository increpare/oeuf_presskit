What is Oeuf?

🕹️ Simple controls

Just roll and jump!

👯 Explore co-op online

Play with your friends online!

🛠️ FANCY LEVEL EDITOR!

Build and share your own levels.

🏭 Steam Workshop

Share your levels with the community!

☔ Calm, rainy atmosphere

No enemies, no timers; take your time and soak up the atmosphere.

⏱️ Playtime

5-10 hours in the main game for a new player, with a large bonus level included (and more still on the Steam Workshop!).

🎵 Original SOUNDTRACK

1 hour of music in the base game, with 3 hours of extra music for people who want more choice when making their own levels :)
