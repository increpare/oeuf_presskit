Description

Oeuf is an atmospheric 3D precision platformer from increpare (creator of Stephen's Sausage Roll and many other games) where you are an egg.

  • You've fallen out of your nest in a storm and you're trying to get back up.
  • The game has ended up as something of a precision platformer, but the heart of it is more the location and the mood.
  • It has a built on top of a powerful map editor, which is included with the game. It's very easy to make your map and invite other people to come join you on steam.
  • With the online play, you can join friends coop in the main game, or explore other worlds.

History

I started this at a game-jam in May of 2025 - a friend was visiting Berlin, and we decided both to make games around the theme EGG. We both ended up working on 3D platformers - his one turned into a freeware game https://terrycavanagh.itch.io/egg, and mine turned into Oeuf!

I had been working on a framework for another bigger 3D platform game (still WIP - small clip of it here: https://x.com/increpare/status/1908606615709712392/video/1), and thought it might be nice to use it for something smaller. However, Oeuf grew and grew and I've been working on it full-time since then, turning into a reasonably substantial game in its own right.

I hadn't intended to put multiplayer, but I wanted to just try it out, and found it so much fun to bop around with people that I went all-in on steam multiplayer support :)

Features

  • Simple controls: just roll and jump!
  • A single rainy world: explore one continuous landscape as you find your way back.
  • 3D physics-based movement: Roll, jump, and slide around just like a real egg!
  • Calm, rainy atmosphere: No enemies, no timers; you can take your time and soak up the atmosphere.
  • Short and sweet: about three hours playtime for a new player (though it might take longer if you get stuck...)
  • Challenging moments: the game balances gentle bopping about with more challenging segments. Be prepared to sweat as you line up that final jump before the next checkpoint.
  • Original soundtrack: chill for the most part : )
  • Level editor: needs keyboard+mouse.
  • Explore co-op with your friends online!
